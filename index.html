FAQ

Quick Answers

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring the document to be notarized and an acceptable form of identification. Do not sign in advance when the notarial act requires the signature to be witnessed.

Do you travel to jails and hospitals?

Yes, by appointment and subject to the facility's access, identification, scheduling, and visitor requirements.

Do you prepare legal documents?

No. A notary verifies identity and performs authorized notarial acts. A notary who is not an attorney cannot provide legal advice or select legal documents for you.
