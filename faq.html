Frequently Asked Questions

Answers Before Your Appointment

What do I need for notarization?

The original document and acceptable identification are commonly required. The signer must be able to communicate directly with the notary and meet the requirements for the requested notarial act.

Can you come to my home or office?

Yes. Mobile appointments can be scheduled at homes, offices, businesses, and other agreed locations.

Do you visit jails and detention centers?

Yes, when permitted by the facility. Access, visitor approval, document handling, identification, and scheduling requirements vary by facility.

Do you visit hospitals, hospice, and assisted living?

Yes. The signer must be able to meet the requirements for the requested notarization. Facility policies may also apply.

Can you notarize real estate and loan documents?

Signing services can be offered for eligible real estate and loan packages. Confirm that the owner provides loan-signing services before publishing this claim.

Can you tell me which document I need?

No. A notary who is not an attorney cannot provide legal advice, select legal documents, or explain your legal rights. Contact an attorney or the document recipient for legal questions.
