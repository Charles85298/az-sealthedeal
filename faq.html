Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about mobile notary appointments, identification, documents, facility visits and scheduling.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring the document that needs notarization and acceptable identification for each signer. Additional requirements can depend on the document and notarial act.

Should I sign the document before the appointment?

Do not pre-sign a document when the required notarial act calls for signing in the notary's presence. If you are unsure, leave the signature area unsigned until the appointment.

Can you meet me at my home or office?

Yes. Mobile appointments can be requested for appropriate homes, offices and other meeting locations within the service area.

Do you visit hospitals and care facilities?

Appointments can be requested for hospitals, hospice, rehabilitation, skilled nursing and assisted-living facilities, subject to access requirements.

Can you visit a jail or detention facility?

Visits can be requested, but scheduling and completion depend on the facility's access, identification and appointment rules.

Are you certified for real estate and loan signings?

Yes. The owner is an NNA Certified Notary Signing Agent and a Real Estate Certified Loan Signing Agent and provides professional signing support for eligible real estate and loan documents.

Can a notary provide legal advice?

No. Notary service does not include legal advice, selecting legal documents or document preparation.
